About Thaalam

Thaalam Performing Arts is a non-profit organization dedicated to preserving, promoting, and celebrating Tamil percussion arts and cultural heritage.

Our mission is to keep the ancient rhythms alive by teaching traditional drumming techniques, organizing performances, and creating a vibrant community of artists and enthusiasts who share a passion for Tamil cultural expressions.

Through education, performance, and cultural exchange, we aim to pass on these invaluable traditions to future generations while making them accessible and relevant to contemporary audiences.

The Parai

Ancient Tamil Percussion

The Parai is a traditional Tamil percussion instrument with deep cultural and historical significance, dating back thousands of years in Tamil Nadu.

This frame drum, typically played with two sticks, produces powerful, resonant rhythms that have been integral to Tamil celebrations, ceremonies, and cultural events throughout history.
